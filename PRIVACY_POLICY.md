# Split Basket — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Split Basket ("the app") is made by Ossip Labs (Josh Ossip). It helps groups
of people split shared grocery bills. This policy explains what the app
collects, why, and how to get it deleted.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Email address and password** (through Firebase Authentication) | To sign you in. Your password is handled by Google Firebase; we never see it. |
| **Your name/username** | So the people you share baskets with can find and recognise you. |
| **Baskets, items, prices and charges** | This is the app's job: working out who owes what. Members of a basket see that basket's contents. |
| **Receipt photos** you choose to upload | To read the items and prices off the receipt. |
| **Push-notification token** | To tell you when someone adds a charge or a basket changes. |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or advertising ID, and the app
has no ads or third-party analytics.

## Connected stores (Costco)

If you connect Costco in the app's *Connections* tab, you sign in to Costco
inside a Costco web page shown in the app. Your Costco password and session go
only to Costco and stay on your phone. The app reads your recent receipts on
that page and adds the items you choose to a basket. Your Costco credentials
are never sent to us or stored on our servers. Disconnecting Costco, signing
out of Split Basket or deleting your account removes the Costco sign-in from
your phone.

## Who processes your data

- **Google Firebase** (authentication, database, file storage, notifications)
  stores your account and baskets.
- **Google Cloud Vision** and **OpenAI** read uploaded receipt images and
  receipt item names so they can be turned into a list of items. They receive
  only the receipt image or item text, not your account details. OpenAI does
  not use data sent through its API to train its models.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

## How long we keep it

Your account is kept until you delete it. A basket is deleted when its host
finalizes it; the charges it creates are kept until they're marked as paid.
Receipt photos you upload are kept until you delete your account.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: on the *Baskets* tab, tap your profile
picture, then *Delete account*. It's deleted right away. You can also ask for
deletion on the web at <https://splitbasketapp.web.app/delete-account>, and we'll
delete your account within 30 days of your request.

Deleting removes your account, your profile, your friends list, the receipt
photos you uploaded, and baskets only you are in. Items and charges you share
with other people are kept, shown as "Deleted user" instead of your name, so
the other members' totals still add up.

## Children

Split Basket is not directed at children under 13.
