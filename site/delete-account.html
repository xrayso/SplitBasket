Delete your Split Basket account

You can delete your account yourself in the app, right away. If you can't use the app, email us and we'll delete it for you.

In the app

  1. Open Split Basket and go to the Baskets tab.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password, then tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email joshossip10@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, and include your username if you remember it. We'll check the request came from you and delete your account within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What's deleted

  • Your account and sign-in (email address and password)
  • Your profile: username, friend tag and notification token
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Receipt photos you scanned, and the items read from them
  • Baskets that only you are in

What's kept

Items and charges you share with other people stay in their baskets and balances, so their totals still add up. They show as "Deleted user" and are no longer linked to your name or email. You're removed from shared baskets; if you hosted one, another member takes over.

If you connected Costco, that sign-in is stored only on your phone. Deleting your account in the app removes it. Your Costco account itself isn't affected.
